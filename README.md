### StackLabs products

**Appetito** — [appetito.app.br](https://appetito.app.br)
Co-founded Appetito (2022), a restaurant ordering platform that takes iFood orders, payments and subscriptions, and runs a WhatsApp bot that takes and closes orders on its own. Advised on product and architecture from day one; lead backend engineer since Jul 2026.
24 active merchants · 80+ since launch · 50k+ orders · R$ 2.8M GMV.
*On the page:* the public product page, with sign-up and the WhatsApp assistant.

**Excurfly** — [karavana-prototipos.vercel.app](https://karavana-prototipos.vercel.app)
A multi-tenant platform for travel agencies to sell group trips; the prototype runs under Karavana Turismo, its first paying tenant. Rewrote the backend from Go/Gin to TypeScript, Fastify and PostgreSQL as sole engineer; authored 35 ADRs and implemented recurring billing, split payouts and failed-charge recovery against a payment provider.
*On the page:* passenger, operator, company and storefront views, on mobile and desktop, in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

**Rantá** — [motoboys-system.vercel.app](https://motoboys-system.vercel.app)
A dispatch platform for motorcycle couriers, at prototype stage.
*On the page:* dispatch, fleet, courier and customer views, a business view and an audit view.

**Appetito card machine** — [appetito-maquininha-prototipo.vercel.app](https://appetito-maquininha-prototipo.vercel.app)
A point-of-sale app for Appetito restaurants. Prototype; development postponed.
*On the page:* setup, counter and table service, order tickets and the dashboard.

### Own projects

**Cadillac Queue** — [fila-cadillac-prototipos.vercel.app](https://fila-cadillac-prototipos.vercel.app)
Built and licensed a dining-room waitlist to Cadillac Burger: customers join by QR code with no app or login, while a three-step WhatsApp-to-voice fallback keeps failed notifications from blocking the queue.
*On the page:* the guest's QR sign-up, the staff host stand, and the current flow next to the proposed one.

**Cadillac Delivery** — [cadillac-prototipos.vercel.app](https://cadillac-prototipos.vercel.app)
Built and delivered Cadillac Burger's own ordering platform across web and mobile; not deployed after an internal client decision.
*On the page:* the customer app, the order board and the admin panel.

**Poker Blind Timer** — [alma-blinds-poker.vercel.app](https://alma-blinds-poker.vercel.app)
Defined the architecture and domain model and guided its implementation; representing blinds as integer multiples of the smallest chip makes unpayable blinds impossible.
*On the page:* the live app: pick your smallest chip and the blind structure follows.
